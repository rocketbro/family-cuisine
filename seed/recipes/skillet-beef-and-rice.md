A one-pan weeknight dinner. A starter recipe: change it or delete it.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 yellow onion, chopped
- 1 cup rice
- 1 can diced tomatoes, 15 oz
- 1 1/2 cups water
- 1 tsp salt

## Steps

1. Brown the beef and onion in a deep skillet, about 8 minutes. Spoon off any extra fat.
2. Stir in the rice, tomatoes, water and salt, and bring to a boil.
3. Cover, turn the heat to low and cook until the rice is tender, about 20 minutes.
4. Let it sit off the heat for 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork.
