Everything roasts on one pan. A starter recipe: change it or delete it.

## Ingredients

- 2 lb chicken thighs
- 2 lb potatoes, cut in chunks
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp paprika

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 425°F.
2. Toss the potatoes with half the oil and salt on a sheet pan.
3. Rub the chicken with the rest of the oil and salt and the paprika, and nestle it among the potatoes.
4. Roast about 40 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through and the potatoes are browned.
