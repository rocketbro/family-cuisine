Crisp in a dry skillet, ready in the time it takes to melt the cheese. A starter recipe: change it or delete it.

## Ingredients

- 1 can black beans, 15 oz, drained
- 2 cups shredded cheese
- 8 tortillas
- 1/2 cup salsa (optional)

## Steps

1. Mash the beans roughly with a fork.
2. Spread beans over half of each tortilla, add cheese and fold.
3. Cook in a dry skillet over medium heat, about 3 minutes a side, until golden.
4. Cut into wedges and serve with salsa.
