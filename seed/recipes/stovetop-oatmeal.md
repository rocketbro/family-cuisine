A plain pot of oats to dress up at the table. A starter recipe: change it or delete it.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups rolled oats
- 4 cups water
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 2 tbsp honey (optional)
- 2 bananas, sliced (optional)

## Steps

1. Bring the water and salt to a boil in a medium pot.
2. Stir in the oats, turn the heat down and simmer about 5 minutes, stirring now and then.
3. Spoon into bowls and top with the honey and bananas.
