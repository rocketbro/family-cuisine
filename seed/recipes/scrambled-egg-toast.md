Soft eggs on buttered toast. A starter recipe: change it or delete it.

## Ingredients

- 8 eggs
- 2 tbsp butter
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 4 slices bread

## Steps

1. Toast the bread.
2. Beat the eggs with the salt.
3. Melt the butter in a skillet over medium-low heat, add the eggs and stir slowly until just set.
4. Spoon over the toast.
