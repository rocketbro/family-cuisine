A big, forgiving pot that tastes better the next day. A starter recipe: change it or delete it.

## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 yellow onion, chopped
- 2 carrots, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 1/2 cups dried lentils, rinsed
- 1 can diced tomatoes, 28 oz
- 6 cups chicken broth
- 1 tsp salt

## Steps

1. Warm the oil in a large pot and cook the onion and carrots until soft, about 8 minutes.
2. Add the garlic and stir for a minute.
3. Add the lentils, tomatoes, broth and salt. Bring to a boil, then simmer until the lentils are tender, about 30 minutes.
4. Taste and add more salt if it needs it.
